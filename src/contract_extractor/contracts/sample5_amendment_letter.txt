AMENDMENT No. 1 TO SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

From: Northwind Analytics Ltd
To: Brightfield Retail plc

Date: 1 October 2025

Re: Software Subscription Agreement dated 1 April 2025 (the "Agreement")

Dear Tom,

Further to our recent discussions, Northwind Analytics Ltd and Brightfield Retail plc agree to amend the Agreement as follows, with effect from the date of this letter:

1. Clause 3.2 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with: "Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other party not less than sixty (60) days' written notice."

2. The number of named users in clause 1.1 is increased from 250 to 400. The annual subscription fee is increased accordingly to GBP 245,000 from the next renewal invoice.

Except as set out in this letter, the Agreement continues in full force and effect.

Please sign below to confirm Brightfield Retail plc's agreement.

Yours sincerely,
Priya Raman, Chief Executive Officer, Northwind Analytics Ltd

Agreed and accepted: Tom Achebe, Chief Operating Officer, Brightfield Retail plc
