COMPONENT SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Component Supply Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by and among:

KESTREL COMPONENTS INC., a New York corporation ("Buyer");
HALVORSEN MANUFACTURING AS, a Norwegian company ("Seller"); and
KESTREL HOLDINGS CORP., a Delaware corporation and parent of Buyer ("Guarantor").

This Agreement is effective as of the date of the last signature below (the "Effective Date").

1. SUPPLY
Seller shall manufacture and supply precision aluminium housings ("Products") in accordance with purchase orders issued by Buyer and the specifications in Schedule A.

2. PRICE AND PAYMENT
Prices are set out in Schedule B in US dollars. Buyer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days.

3. TERM AND RENEWAL
3.1 The initial term of this Agreement is one (1) year from the Effective Date.
3.2 This Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either Buyer or Seller gives written notice of non-renewal at least six (6) months before the end of the then-current term.

4. GUARANTEE
Guarantor unconditionally guarantees the payment obligations of Buyer under this Agreement.

5. WARRANTY
Seller warrants that Products will conform to the specifications for twenty-four (24) months from delivery.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Except for breach of confidentiality or indemnification obligations, the maximum aggregate liability of each party under this Agreement shall not exceed two million US dollars (USD 2,000,000).

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
